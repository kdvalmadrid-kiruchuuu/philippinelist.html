PALAWAN


Name: palawan
Official Location: Province of Palawan, MIMAROPA Region (Region IV-B), Philippines
Informative Description:
Palawan is an archipelagic province in the Philippines widely known as the country's "Last Ecological Frontier." It is the largest Philippine province by land area, encompassing a long, narrow main island and roughly 1,780 surrounding islands and islets celebrated for dramatic limestone cliffs, turquoise lagoons, lush rainforests, and rich biodiversity. the Philippines, offering relaxation, adventure, and exciting nightlife.
Attractions:
Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park
El Nido (Bacuit Archipelago)
Coron (Calamian Group):
ubbataha Reefs Natural Park
Port Barton & San Vicente
Tabon Caves (Quezon)
Reasons Why It Is Visited:
Unspoiled Beaches & Dramatic Island-Hopping.
World-Class Scuba Diving & Snorkeling
Unique Natural Wonders & Biodiversity


EL NIDO


Name: El Nido
Location: Municipality of El Nido, Northern Palawan, MIMAROPA Region (Region IV-B), Philippines
Informative Description:

A coastal municipality at the northern tip of mainland Palawan serving as the gateway to the Bacuit Archipelago. It is world-famous for towering black marble and limestone karst cliffs, emerald lagoons, and secluded white-sand coves.

Attractions:


Big Lagoon & Small Lagoon (Miniloc Island)
Nacpan Beach
Secret Beach & Hidden Beach (Matinloc Island)
Taraw Cliff
Reasons Why It Is Visited:
Dramatic Island-Hopping Scenery
To experience island hopping and snorkeling.
Pristine Beaches & Crystal Waters
Vibrant Beachfront Dining & Resort Options


CORON


Name: Coron
Location: Municipality of Coron (and Busuanga Island), Calamian Islands, Northern Palawan, MIMAROPA Region (Region IV-B), Philippines
Informative Description:
A premier island destination in the Calamian Group of Islands in northern Palawan, celebrated for crystal-clear freshwater lakes, dramatic limestone cliffs, and historic underwater wrecks. Attractions:
Kayangan Lake
Barracuda Lake
Twin Lagoon
Coron Bay WWII Shipwrecks
Mount Tapyas & Maquinit Hot Spring
Reasons Why It Is Visited:
World-Renowned Wreck Diving & Freediving
Iconic Freshwater & Brackish Lakes
White-Sand Island Escapes


BALABAC


Name: Balabac
Location: Municipality of Balabac, Southern Palawan, MIMAROPA Region (Region IV-B), Philippines
Informative Description:
A remote, off-the-beaten-path group of over 30 islands at the southernmost tip of Palawan, renowned for untouched sandbars, neon-turquoise shallow waters, and rare wildlife.

Attractions:


Onok Island
Patawan Island
Mansalangan Sandbar
Candaraman Island & Starfish Sandbar
Reasons Why It Is Visited:
Untouched, Crowd-Free Paradise
To enjoy breathtaking panoramic views.
Stunning Sandbars & Neon-Blue Waters
Unique Endemic Wildlife.
